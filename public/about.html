About FII & DII Data

Who runs this site, where the numbers come from and how they are prepared.

What this site is

FII & DII Data is a free dashboard that shows, every trading evening, how much Foreign Institutional Investors (FII/FPI) and Domestic Institutional Investors (DII) bought and sold in Indian equities, how they are positioned in derivatives, and which sectors foreign money is moving into or out of.

Where the data comes from

  • Daily cash flows: NSE's provisional FII/DII trading activity, published after market close (about 6–7 PM IST).
  • F&O positioning: NSE participant-wise open interest, published later the same evening.
  • Sector rotation: NSDL's fortnightly FPI sector-wise investment report.
  • Custodian-confirmed FPI figures: NSDL daily FPI trends, which follow the exchange numbers by a day.
  • Index quotes: Nifty 50, Sensex and India VIX via Yahoo Finance.
  • Bulk and block deals: NSE large-deal disclosures.

How to read it

All cash amounts are in Indian rupees, crore (₹ Cr). Green means net buying and red means net selling. Exchange figures are provisional on the evening they are published; the dashboard marks them as such, and says so plainly if the feed falls behind.

Scores and labels such as Market Strength, F&O Sentiment or “Bullish/Bearish” are simple, rule-based summaries of the published numbers. They describe what institutions did; they are not forecasts or recommendations.
